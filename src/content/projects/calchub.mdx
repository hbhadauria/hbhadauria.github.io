CalcHub (`com.hsb.calchub`) is an offline-first, zero-analytics Android app with **34 calculators across 6 categories** (investment, loan, retirement, tax, trading, other). It ships with no network access (`INTERNET` is not declared in the merged release manifest). CalcHub is open-sourced under the MIT license on GitHub, and the **v3.0.0 revamp is live on Google Play**.

## Timeline

| Version | When | What changed |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **v1.0.0** | Nov 2025 | Prototype built in about 24 hours with Gemini inside Antigravity. Material 3, MVVM. Live on Google Play. |
| **v2.0.0** | 2025-11-30 | Dark neon / glassmorphism redesign, shared calculator scaffold, donut charts, Favorites tab. |
| **v2.1.0** | 2025-12-08 | Open-sourced under the MIT licence. Release keystore and Play credentials kept outside the repository. |
| **v3.0.0** | 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-17 | Full revamp: toolchain upgrade, Expressive Aurora design, Navigation 3, custom charts, hardware-verified CI (**live on Google Play**). |

Read the original story in [From Idea to App Store](/posts/from-idea-to-app-store-how-i-built-a-mobile-app-in-24-hours-without-coding/).

New in v3.0.0: a Continue banner on Home, a four-tab Tools Lab (Utilities, Compare, Vault, Formulas), and six upgraded showcase calculators, plus two extra skins (Ledger Paper and Dot Matrix).

<DecisionRecord id="ADR-02" title="Dependency injection" decision="A 35-line manual AppContainer instead of Hilt / KSP">
  Zero codegen friction on Kotlin 2.4.21 with JVM 25. The app only needs a handful of repositories.
</DecisionRecord>

<DecisionRecord id="ADR-03" title="Charts" decision="Zero-dependency charts built on Compose Canvas + PathMeasure">
  Interactive wealth-growth, loan-amortization and FIRE-timeline charts without an external charting library.
</DecisionRecord>
