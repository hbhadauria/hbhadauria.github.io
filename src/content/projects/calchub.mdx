CalcHub (`com.hsb.calchub`) is an offline-first, zero-analytics Android app with **42 calculators across 6 categories** (investment, loan, retirement, tax, trading, other). It ships with no network access (`INTERNET` is not declared in the merged release manifest). CalcHub is open-sourced under the MIT license on GitHub: **`v3.0.0` is live on Google Play**, and **`v3.1.0` is coming soon**.

## Timeline

| Version | When | What changed |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **v1.0.0** | Nov 2025 | Prototype built in about 24 hours with Gemini inside Antigravity. Material 3, MVVM. Live on Google Play. |
| **v2.0.0** | 2025-11-30 | Dark neon / glassmorphism redesign, shared calculator scaffold, donut charts, Favorites tab. |
| **v2.1.0** | 2025-12-08 | Open-sourced under the MIT licence. Release keystore and Play credentials kept outside the repository. |
| **v3.0.0** | 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-17 | Full revamp: toolchain upgrade, Expressive Aurora design, Navigation 3, custom charts, hardware-verified CI (**live on Google Play**). |
| **v3.1.0** | 2026-11-22 | Math audit across 30 engines, 8 new calculators (42 total) + CTC Salary mode, Minimal Black theme, shared result-first scaffolds, and CI/static-analysis hardening (**coming soon**). |

## How AI-Assisted Engineering Evolved

CalcHub is the running case study behind the **AI-Assisted Engineering** series—showing how AI workflows mature from rapid generation to strict, self-verifying engineering loops:

1. **[Part 1 — Idea to App Store in 24h](/posts/from-idea-to-app-store-how-i-built-a-mobile-app-in-24-hours-without-coding/) (`v1.0.0`)**: Prompt-driven MVP velocity to validate the product and ship quickly.
2. **[Part 2 — Redesign with Strict Quality Gates](/posts/calchub-v3-redesign-with-ai/) (`v3.0.0`)**: HTML mockups before Kotlin code, ADRs for Navigation 3 and manual DI, plus Robolectric and real-device Espresso verification.
3. **[Part 3 — Auditing AI Math & Taming Duplication](/posts/calchub-v3-1-math-audit-and-polish/) (`v3.1.0`)**: Line-by-line domain math audit, HTML-first Minimal Black preview, and using `detekt`, PMD CPD, `actionlint`, and `zizmor` as automated feedback loops for AI agents.

`v3.0.0` introduced a Continue banner on Home, a four-tab Tools Lab (Utilities, Compare, Vault, Formulas), six upgraded showcase calculators, and two extra skins (Ledger Paper and Dot Matrix). `v3.1.0` audited and fixed 8 formula edge cases, added an inflation-adjustment toggle, expanded the catalog from 34 to **42 calculators** (adding Capital Gains FY26, Goal SIP, Loan Prepayment, Credit Card Payoff, FIRE / Coast-FIRE, Buy vs. Rent, Spend vs. Invest, Freelance Tax Sec 44ADA, and CTC Salary mode), added the **Minimal Black** theme, and unified screens on result-first shared scaffolds.

<DecisionRecord id="ADR-02" title="Dependency injection" decision="A 35-line manual AppContainer instead of Hilt / KSP">
  Zero codegen friction on Kotlin 2.4.21 with JVM 25. The app only needs a handful of repositories.
</DecisionRecord>

<DecisionRecord id="ADR-03" title="Charts" decision="Zero-dependency charts built on Compose Canvas + PathMeasure">
  Interactive wealth-growth, loan-amortization and FIRE-timeline charts without an external charting library.
</DecisionRecord>

<DecisionRecord id="ADR-04" title="Calculator layouts" decision="Result-first ordering on shared Compose scaffolds">
  Standardized all 42 screens to `Result -> Inputs -> Charts` on `CalculatorScrollScaffold`, `StandardInvestmentCalculatorLayout`, and `StandardResultsCalculatorLayout`, removing roughly 950 net lines of duplicated screen code flagged by PMD CPD.
</DecisionRecord>
