In [Part 1](/posts/from-idea-to-app-store-how-i-built-a-mobile-app-in-24-hours-without-coding/) I shipped CalcHub in about 24 hours. A year later (Nov 15–17, 2026) I did the opposite: a deliberate, gated full revamp to **v3.0.0**.

## Why revamp after shipping in 24 hours

v2 was a dark neon / glassmorphism UI on legacy navigation and an aging toolchain. v3.0.0 replaced it with **Expressive Aurora** (Material 3 Expressive, solid tonal surfaces, no blur across API 26–37) and moved to Kotlin 2.4.21 with Temurin JDK 25.

## Decisions as ADRs

<DecisionRecord id="ADR-01" title="Navigation" decision="Jetpack Navigation 3 only">
  NavDisplay with serializable keys, predictive back, and a list-detail scene strategy: single pane below 600dp, side-by-side on tablets and foldables.
</DecisionRecord>

<DecisionRecord id="ADR-02" title="Dependency injection" decision="A 35-line manual AppContainer over Hilt">
  No KSP codegen friction on Kotlin 2.4.21 + JVM 25.
</DecisionRecord>

<DecisionRecord id="ADR-03" title="Charts" decision="Compose Canvas + PathMeasure, no chart library">
  Scrubbable wealth-growth, amortization and FIRE-timeline charts with haptic feedback.
</DecisionRecord>

The release tag and source are published on [GitHub](https://github.com/hbhadauria/CalcHub), and `v3.0.0` is **live on Google Play**.
